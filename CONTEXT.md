# Context

Glossary for the Vanguard client intelligence effort. Terms resolve here as they crystallize; nothing else lives in this file.

## Terms

- **Vanguard** — the subject firm: a client-owned (mutual) asset manager headquartered in the US. The firm this intelligence site is about. Not publicly traded; publishes no firm-level financial statements (no income statement, balance sheet, or cash-flow statement — verified in `assets/01-vanguard-public-disclosures.md`); publishes headline statistics (AUM through H1 2022, client counts, cost ratios, fund counts, crew) and fund-level financial statements.
- **Client intelligence** — an internal reference knowledge base about a firm, maintained to support a team's understanding of its financial health, competitive position, and improvement opportunities. Internal, not client-facing.
- **Fact base** — the structured collection of Vanguard and peer financial data (5 years, public sources only) that the analysis and site are built on.
- **Data-rich competitor fact base** — the planned local-first authoritative database for the site: five fiscal years of facts for Vanguard and the current peer set (BlackRock, Fidelity, State Street/SSGA, Invesco, Amundi), with metric definitions, citations, verification and collection audit data. It retains non-comparable published facts with explicit flags rather than silently normalizing them.
- **Aggregator lead** — a fact located through a reputable public aggregator. It cannot be published as a database observation until corroborated by a primary source or a second independent public source.
- **Metric** — a quantitative measure tracked for Vanguard and its peers (e.g., AUM, number of clients, revenue, expenses, RoE).
- **Canonical metric taxonomy** — the approved expanded metric families: core financial statements; scale and flows; profitability and efficiency; and business mix and segments. A metric enters the canonical set only when it covers Vanguard plus at least two peers across at least three of five years; published non-comparable facts remain stored and are shown with warnings.
- **AUM** — assets under management.
- **Peer set** — the group of firms Vanguard is benchmarked against, defined by membership rules agreed in the peer set decision (recorded in `issues/04-grilling-peer-set.md`): audited financials for the 5-year window (10-K or equivalent, with a documented exception for high-comparability private firms), AUM scale floor, and business-mix overlap (index/ETF + retirement + advice). Current membership: see the decision.
- **Voluntary-data core member** — a peer admitted to the core set without audited firm financials (currently: Fidelity). Every Fidelity metric is labeled voluntary + source + date; Fidelity is excluded from audited-metric comparisons (e.g., RoE) and shown as voluntary side data instead.
- **RoE (Return on Equity)** — net income over equity; the profitability lens for the analysis. Not directly computable for Vanguard from published data (no net income or equity published — verified in `assets/01-vanguard-public-disclosures.md`); the analysis uses an approved proxy (e.g., fund-level advisory fees minus estimated costs), secondary estimates clearly labeled, or a cost-ratio reframing. Market-derived for listed peers; the comparability caveat is part of the analysis.
- **RoE tree** — a decomposition of RoE into driver nodes (income-statement drilldown, decided ticket 05: RoE = net income ÷ average equity; left branch revenue → operating expenses → operating income → net income; right branch average equity), shown across the 5 years with a year selector (latest fiscal year as the primary view); every node a published 10-K line for peers or an explicit "not published" gap for Vanguard, the operating-expenses node carrying the published cost ratio as a labeled ratio note.
- **Product taxonomy** — the canonical catalog the site shows, decided ticket 07: six categories in display order **funds, ETFs, retirement, brokerage, advice, institutional**, at offering-level depth (headline offerings with sourced facts; fund-level granularity excluded at ticket 03). The catalog is the product view of Vanguard's business.
- **Line of business (LoB)** — Vanguard's operating lines, canonical since ticket 07: **investment management, retirement, brokerage, advice** (in that order). The financial-comparison view of the same business the product taxonomy catalogs. Vanguard publishes no segment financials — every Vanguard LoB cell renders as an explicit "not published" gap, never derived. Catalog-to-LoB mapping (ticket 07): funds + ETFs + institutional → investment management; retirement → retirement; brokerage → brokerage; advice → advice.
- **Benchmarking** — comparing Vanguard's metrics against the peer set.
- **RoE comparisons** — the comparison views (ticket 06/16): a peer-set RoE table (rows = firms Vanguard-first, columns = FY2021–25, ownership caveat + Fidelity voluntary-side-data note) and a line-of-business-vs-industry panel. The LoB comparison uses the canonical 4-line model (investment management / retirement / brokerage / advice, ticket 07); Vanguard segment RoE renders as explicit "not published" gaps (never derived proxies). Each LoB benchmarks against the industry it competes in, represented by peer-set firms only (investment management → asset management: BlackRock, Invesco, Amundi; retirement → retirement recordkeeping: State Street + Fidelity voluntary; brokerage → brokerage & trading: Fidelity voluntary; advice → wealth/advice: Fidelity voluntary), every representative cell rendering "Pending collection" until ticket 17; non-core industry representatives (e.g., Schwab) deferred to ticket 17. No year selector on the page — all cells are gaps and the RoE tree owns year drilldown.
- **Improvement opportunity** — an area where the analysis indicates Vanguard can improve, surfaced for internal reference; the improvement lens is broad business performance, with technology one possible lever among many.
- **Analysis pipeline** — the LLM research process (ticket 17) that produces the "how is Vanguard faring" narrative and the named improvement opportunities, each connected to its evidence metrics, with the improvement lens stated; the seeded pipeline output lives in `src/data/analysis.ts` and is presented on the home page. Peer-relative reads are honestly labeled as awaiting peer data — real peer collection is a separate follow-up.
- **Refresh pipeline** — the documented, re-runnable quarterly process (ticket 20, runbook in `docs/runbooks/quarterly-refresh.md`): data collection → fact base → re-analysis → validate (`npm run refresh:validate`, the gate in `src/lib/refresh.ts`) → stamp data-as-of (`site.dataAsOf` in `src/lib/site.ts`) → redeploy → verify. Provenance intact on every run: full (metric, firm) coverage, every point sourced, gaps stay gaps (nothing invented), analysis grounded in real headline metrics, and the dated marker visible site-wide.
- **Chatbot** — the site's interactive Q&A on the fact base and analysis (ticket 09/18): a deterministic retrieval engine, not a live LLM API call. Queries are matched against a static intent registry (metric queries, 5-year trend queries, benchmarking, RoE-tree/profitability, improvement reads, ownership/company context); the best-scoring intent above threshold answers by rendering facts through the fact-base accessors, so every figure and source link is a fact-base literal. Refusals are checked before intent matching and are fixed literal strings: investment-advice words, regulatory/filing advice, non-fact-base topics, forecasts, and legal. Benchmarking and RoE answers append the mutual-vs-listed ownership caveat verbatim. The engine runs in-browser on the /chatbot view — no API route, no network.
- **Cost ratio** — Vanguard's published expense measure: asset-weighted average US fund expenses as a share of prior-year average net US assets (0.09% 2020–21 → 0.08% 2022–23 → 0.07% 2024–25). Distinct from the 0.06% fund-lineup average cited in 2026 press materials (different measure — compare like-for-like before quoting). One of the 5 headline metrics (decision: ticket 03).
