# Grilling: evidence & quality policy (simplified)

## Question

What is the simple, best-effort evidence policy for the two new sections?

User directives:
- Strong sources (firm disclosures, regulatory filings, first-party platform pages) stand alone.
- Weak/secondary sources (media, interviews, third-party trackers, social) must be triangulated — ≥2 independent corroborating sources with compatible definitions and periods.
- "Good quality data according to best effort" — the earlier "triangulated secondary" tag scheme felt hacky; simplify.
- Plain language: "source citation", not "provenance".
- Nothing unsourced; gaps stay gaps where nothing citable was found.

Draft recommended answer:
- Every number renders with its source(s), retrieval date, and as-of date.
- Two quality tiers: **Primary** (firm/regulatory/first-party platform) and **Corroborated** (≥2 independent secondary sources).
- A single weak source is not shown as a fact.
- Derived rows (growth %, % of AUM, revenue/AUM) compute from sourced inputs with the formula disclosed; a derived cell inherits the weakest tier of its inputs. (RoE is excluded entirely — user directive.)
- Decide the exact display treatment: a one-line "sourced / corroborated" note with citation links vs. a full citation block.
