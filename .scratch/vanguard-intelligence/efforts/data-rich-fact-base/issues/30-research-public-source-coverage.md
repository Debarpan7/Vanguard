## Question

For Vanguard, BlackRock, Fidelity, State Street/SSGA, Invesco, and Amundi, which internet-accessible primary sources and corroborating public sources can supply five fiscal years of the candidate expanded financial and operating metrics?

Inventory each source’s publisher, URL, reporting cadence, metric/period coverage, data format, access or rate-limit constraints, currency/accounting basis, firm-versus-segment scope, and evidence quality. Identify which candidate metrics can satisfy a defensible coverage floor and which must remain explicit gaps, voluntary side data, or display-only/non-comparable facts.

This research does not choose the canonical metrics, database engine, or ingestion design. It provides the factual source landscape those decisions require.

## Answer

Research asset: [`assets/01-public-source-coverage.md`](../assets/01-public-source-coverage.md).

BlackRock and Invesco can supply audited five-year consolidated series through SEC filings and XBRL; Amundi can support regulated EUR/IFRS annual-report coverage pending direct FY2021–25 extraction. State Street requires a five-year Investment Management segment-note review before its data represents SSGA economics. Vanguard lacks corporate profitability/equity disclosures, while Fidelity remains voluntary operating side data with no auditable FMR financial series. The asset records source details and the remaining evidence risks.
