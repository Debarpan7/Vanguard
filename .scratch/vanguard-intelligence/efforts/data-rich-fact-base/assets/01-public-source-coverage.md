# Public-source coverage for the current peer set

- **Date of research:** 2026-08-13
- **Scope:** Internet-accessible first-party, regulatory, and corroborating public sources for FY2021-FY2025 expanded financial and operating facts for Vanguard, BlackRock, Fidelity, State Street/SSGA, Invesco, and Amundi. This is a source-landscape asset; it neither selects the canonical metric taxonomy nor proposes ingestion implementation.
- **Owned by:** `.scratch/vanguard-intelligence/efforts/data-rich-fact-base/issues/30-research-public-source-coverage.md`

## Method and evidence legend

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ **VERIFIED-FROM-URL** | The cited publisher or regulator route was read in this research session. |
| 📄 **PRIMARY-DOCUMENT** | A primary filing/report is publicly linked, but its detailed tables were not extracted in this session. |
| ⚠️ **PRIMARY-ROUTE-NEEDS-MANUAL-COLLECTION** | The official route is public, but the research fetcher could not extract it; collect in a browser and preserve the downloaded document URL, hash, and page/table citation. |
| 🚫 **NOT-A-SOURCE-FOR-THIS-METRIC** | The entity does not publicly publish the required firm-level fact; do not fill the gap with inference. |

The recommended evidence hierarchy is: regulatory filing or regulated annual report; issuer-hosted copy of that filing; issuer results/AUM release for facts not in the statements; then a reputable secondary or aggregator route. A secondary source may support an explicitly `unverified` display/context fact when its limitations are retained, but does not by itself meet the evidence standard for audited or like-for-like comparison.

## Executive coverage finding

The peer set does **not** support one universal five-year, firm-level metric panel. BlackRock and Invesco have FY2021-FY2025 USD, US-GAAP consolidated financial coverage from 10-Ks, with issuer annual-report pages and SEC filing/XBRL routes as complementary collection paths. [BlackRock annual reports](https://ir.blackrock.com/financials/annual-reports-and-proxy/default.aspx) list FY2021-FY2025 reports; [Invesco's investor-relations page](https://ir.invesco.com/financial-information/annual-reports/default.aspx) links annual reports, earnings releases, AUM releases, and SEC filings. State Street has the same regulated parent-company coverage, but its asset-management competitor is SSGA within the Investment Management segment, so consolidated bank results must be marked parent scope and cannot stand in for SSGA economics. [State Street's SEC company page](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=93751&owner=exclude) identifies it as the listed registrant and exposes its annual-report category.

Vanguard has five-year coverage for selected operating facts and cost ratios, but no firm-level income statement, balance sheet, or cash-flow statement. [Vanguard facts and figures](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/why-vanguard/who-we-are/facts-and-figures.html) identifies its investor-owned structure and publishes funds, investor count, expense ratio, and employee count, while [SEC IAPD](https://adviserinfo.sec.gov/firm/summary/105958) provides a current Form ADV link rather than a corporate financial filing. Fidelity is private and its own page says it has been privately held since 1946; it publishes operational scale facts but not audited FMR LLC financial statements. [Fidelity: We are Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/about-fidelity/our-company) Therefore, Vanguard and Fidelity financial profitability/equity fields remain explicit gaps, and Fidelity facts are voluntary side data.

Amundi's public annual-report route is its Universal Registration Document (URD) / annual financial report and regulated information rather than a US 10-K. Its financial-information page is [officially published by Amundi](https://about.amundi.com/Shareholders/Financial-information), and [Euronext's issuer news route](https://live.euronext.com/en/products/equities/company-news/FR0004125920-XPAR) is the corroborating market-disclosure index. Those pages did not yield machine-extractable text in this session, so a collector must verify each FY2021-FY2025 URD directly. The pre-existing peer-universe research verified Amundi's reporting regime as EUR/IFRS; retain both fields without silent conversion or US-GAAP normalization.

## Collection controls and limitations

1. **SEC is the principal machine-readable source for US public peers.** Its APIs are unauthenticated JSON, cover submissions and filing XBRL, and update as filings are disseminated. The SEC also states that standard XBRL API facts are entity-wide and use non-custom taxonomies, so custom, segment, AUM, and KPI metrics still require the underlying filing/table. [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/sec-api-documentation) ✅
2. **Rate limiting is a real collection constraint.** The SEC requires a declared User-Agent and limits automated access to 10 requests per second. Batch collection should throttle below that ceiling, cache documents, record CIK/accession, and use the nightly bulk archives only where their size is justified. [SEC developer FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions#code-support) ✅
3. **Issuer pages are evidence and a fallback, not a stable API contract.** BlackRock's archive supplied direct FY2021-FY2025 report links; Invesco's current IR page links annual reports/AUM/earnings; State Street and Amundi public routes resisted extraction. Store a retrieval timestamp and document hash for PDFs/HTML, and retain the regulatory alternative for each issuer.
4. **Accounting and scope are collection fields, not presentation afterthoughts.** BlackRock, State Street and Invesco are USD/US-GAAP consolidated issuers; Amundi is EUR/IFRS. State Street's asset-manager relevance is limited by the difference between parent and Investment Management/SSGA scope. Vanguard is investor-owned and Fidelity private, so neither should acquire invented equity/profit facts through normalization.

## Unresolved factual risks for follow-up collection

- Read and catalog each FY2021-FY2025 Vanguard ADV (historical PDF/CSV/XML), then establish whether the reported regulatory AUM has a stable definition and date fit for the desired fact-base period. It cannot establish corporate profitability.
- Retrieve and inspect all five Amundi URDs/annual financial reports. Confirm exact titles, permanent document URLs, auditor/IFRS basis, EUR unit scale, consolidation perimeter, and any changes to adjusted versus IFRS measures before declaring five-year coverage complete.
- Read the State Street segment notes for every year before asserting an SSGA series. Confirm the Investment Management metric definition, whether a measure reconciles to consolidated GAAP, and whether the segment reporting changed.
- Treat BlackRock's EDGAR entity-name change as an identifier-resolution check: bind the series to issuer/report/CIK/accession and validate that the filing is BlackRock's consolidated annual report before automatic XBRL extraction.
- Verify Fidelity historical voluntary metrics only from dated Fidelity releases or archived first-party pages. Do not infer a time series from current rounded web figures.

**Bottom line:** A defensible expanded fact base can have audited/regulated core financial facts for BlackRock, Invesco, and Amundi; parent-level facts plus cautiously isolated segment facts for State Street/SSGA; voluntary operating side data for Fidelity; and public operating/cost facts plus regulatory ADV facts for Vanguard. It cannot honestly promise a common five-year firm-level profitability and equity panel across all six firms.
