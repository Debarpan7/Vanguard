# 10 — Prototype: site structure and views

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 06

## Question

What does the site look like — its structure and views?

Raise the fidelity of the discussion with a cheap, concrete prototype (per the /prototype skill). It should sketch:
- **Information architecture**: dashboard, metrics views, products & services, competitor benchmarking, RoE tree drilldown, RoE comparisons, chatbot — and how they link.
- **Key view behaviors**: how the RoE tree drilldown and the comparison views behave.
- **Content scaffolding**: where the metric set (03) and comparison design (06) land on the page.

Link the prototype as an asset. The point is to react to the shape, not to build the production site.
